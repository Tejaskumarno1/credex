## FAQ

### 1. Is SpendLens really free?

Yes, completely free. No credit card, no sign-up required. SpendLens is built by Credex, which makes money selling discounted AI infrastructure credits to companies with high AI spend. This tool helps us find those companies — you get a free audit, and if your spend is significant enough, we might reach out about savings.

### 2. Do you store my data?

Your form data stays in your browser (localStorage) while you're filling it out. When you submit, we store an anonymized version of your tool selection and savings — no personal information. If you choose to save your report, we'll ask for your email. We never sell or share your data.

### 3. How accurate are the recommendations?

Our audit engine uses official pricing from each vendor's pricing page, verified weekly. We recommend downgrades and alternatives based on your team size and use case. However, we can't account for custom enterprise agreements or negotiated rates — use our recommendations as a starting point for internal discussions.

### 4. What tools do you analyze?

Currently: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI), Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini (Google), and Windsurf. We're adding more tools regularly. If you'd like us to add a tool, let us know.

### 5. What if I'm already spending optimally?

We'll tell you that too! Not every audit finds savings. If your stack is well-optimized, we'll confirm it — no pressure to change anything. You'll also get the option to receive alerts if pricing changes could affect your setup.
